    FAQ

    Good questions,
    straight answers.

    How much does an epoxy floor cost?

    It depends on the size of the space, the condition of your concrete, the finish and any extras. Use the calculator above for an example range, and we will give you a firm price after a free visit.

    How long does the job take?

    Most garages take a few days from prep to final coat. Larger or more detailed jobs take longer, and we give you a schedule up front.

    When can I walk and drive on it?

    Usually light foot traffic the next day and vehicles after a few days, once it is fully cured. We confirm the exact timing for your finish.

    How long will it last?

    A well prepped, professionally installed floor can last for many years in a home garage. Heavy commercial use, sun exposure and upkeep all play a part.

    Will it hold up in Florida heat and humidity?

    That is what we plan for. We test the slab for moisture, grind the surface, use a UV-stable topcoat and install when conditions are right.

    Is it slippery when wet?

    Glossy floors can be slick, so we offer an anti-slip texture for garages, ramps and pool decks.

    Do I need to empty the space first?

    Yes, we just ask that the area is cleared before we arrive. We handle the grinding and repairs, and we are happy to talk through what needs to move.

    How do I keep it clean?

    Sweep and mop with a mild cleaner. No wax and no harsh acids needed.
